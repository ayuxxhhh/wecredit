Customer Profile

Financially Invisible,
Not Illiterate.

Our real user is someone who has been made to feel like a bad bet by the formal banking system. Low CIBIL, informal income, first-generation urban. What they want is not just a loan. They want to know: am I even the kind of person who qualifies?

The 29-Year-Old Executive Works in a logistics firm in Pune, earning ₹28,000/month. Her income is real but irregular. Tried an app, got rejected after entering all details. Felt stupid. Never told anyone.
The First-Gen Urban Trader Runs massive volume through UPI but lacks 3 years of audited ITRs. Left a bank branch feeling judged by an officer asking for documents he couldn't produce.

The Enemy

"Credit is not for people like me."

Our combat is not against a specific corporate entity. It's against an internal systemic lie absorbed after years of rejection signals. We don't combat this by saying "everyone qualifies"—that's a lie. We combat it by delivering the truth upfront.

Our Stance: Knowing where you stand is the first step, not a judgment.

Resolving
Contradictions

A brand promise means nothing if operations contradict it. Here is how we align our actions with the Dignity Manifesto.

The Proof Point

Soft Eligibility Checks

The "here is where you stand" moment must happen before the "please enter all your details" moment. We let users see which lenders are likely to approve them based on a soft pull that protects their CIBIL score.

"If a user learns she has a 70% match with two lenders before formally applying, she never has the surprise rejection experience. That is our moat."

The Commitment Accountability

Closing the Response Gap

Silence on complaints is catastrophic. Public silence broadcasts that our promise of credit dignity ends the moment things get operationally inconvenient.

We commit to answering every user, regardless of their eligibility status. Dignity is not transactional.
The Clarity

Removing the "Unbiased" Lie

Transparency is right, but "unbiased" is a structural claim the aggregator model cannot support. We don't feign neutrality.

Our Defensible Promise:

"We show you your full picture." Honesty over marketing buzzwords.

The Anti-Pattern Urgent Action Required

Killing Fatafatloans

A brand resting on "informed decisions before you commit" cannot run a sub-brand marketing the opposite state of mind (urgency & desperation).

The Trust Grenade: Under one umbrella, urgency destroys the dignity promise. We ring-fence or eliminate predatory urgency tactics.
